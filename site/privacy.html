What the game stores

Your progress, settings and in-game coins are stored only on your device. The game has no account and no servers, and we do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and information about your device and how you interact with ads, in order to show and measure ads, limit how often you see them, and prevent fraud. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before any ads are requested, and on iPhone and iPad it asks whether apps may track you. If you decline, ads are not personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Ad privacy choices, and in your device's privacy settings.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Rewarded ads

Watching an ad is always optional. You receive the in-game reward only if the ad plays to the end. Every room can be completed without watching ads.

Analytics

The game does not use a third-party analytics service.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of personal data. Because we do not hold your game data, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google. You can also contact us at the email above.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
